> **Study with freedom. Stay consistent. Reflect deeper.**

**Qurus** is a calm, focused reading and listening space built for everyday life. It offers an unobstructed way to explore the Quran verse by verse—pairing original Arabic recitations with line-by-line spoken translations in 5 languages, an intelligent motivation engine to protect your streak without burnout, private voice and written reflection notes, and background playback for when you are on the move.

## The Story Behind Qurus

### The Dilemma: A Question in the Palm of My Hand
I wanted a way to read and understand the Quran directly from its source with a clear translation. But every time I tried to start, traditional entry points felt heavy and intimidating.

Dense academic commentaries, rigid study setups, and multi-volume texts felt out of touch with how we actually live today. When you can't keep up with that level of formal study, it is easy to feel frustrated and give up.

At the same time, look at how seamless our modern tools are. Apps like Spotify, Instagram, and YouTube rest effortlessly in our hands. Whenever we have two idle minutes—waiting in line, sitting in traffic, or riding the train—our instinct is to reach into our pocket.

It led to a straightforward question:

> *“Why can’t reading and exploring Quran be just as immediate, accessible, and frictionless in our daily lives as the digital habits that already fill our day?”*

## Core Features Built for Everyday Life

### 1. 🌍 Quran Translations & Recitations in 5 Languages
Qurus features complete verse-by-verse text translation and authentic recitation pairing across 5 languages:

* 🇵🇰 **Urdu** — Translation: *Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari* | Recitation: *Shamshad Ali Khan*
* 🇬🇧 **English** — Translation: *Sahih International* | Recitation: *Ibrahim Walk*
* 🇧🇩 **Bengali** — Translation: *Muhiuddin Khan* | Recitation: *Bangla Quran Voice*
* 🇹🇷 **Turkish** — Translation: *Diyanet İşleri* | Recitation: *Diyanet Vakfı*
* 🇫🇷 **French** — Translation: *Muhammad Hamidullah* | Recitation: *Youssouf Leclerc*

Each language dynamically adjusts its typography, font scaling, text direction (RTL/LTR), and authentic audio recitations.

### 2. 🔥 Motivation Engine & Daily Tadabbur Goals
Build a lasting habit without pressure or gamified speedrunning:
- **Streak Countdown**: Real-time indicator showing exactly how much time remains to protect your daily streak.
- **Progressive Streak Savers**: Timely, gentle evening reminders to help you complete your reflection before midnight.
- **Configurable Daily Goals**: Set your daily target (e.g. 5, 10, or 20 Ayahs) according to your own routine.
- **Milestone Celebrations**: Meaningful celebration moments with haptics, particle effects, and reflective wisdom when completing Surahs and reaching streak milestones.
- **Smart Reminder Cancellation**: Once you complete your daily goal, any remaining streak-saver reminders for that day are automatically dismissed.

### 3. 🧭 Structured Quran Journey + Free Exploration
Qurus balances structured reading with unrestricted exploration:
- **Sequential Continuation**: Resume your structured journey exactly where you left off with a single tap.
- **Flexible Checkpoints**: Set any Ayah as your new journey starting point at any time.
- **Free Exploration**: Read, listen, bookmark, annotate, and search across any Surah without altering your primary journey progression.
- **Smart History**: Reviewing past verses no longer disrupts your forward journey milestone.

### 4. 🎧 Synchronized Bilingual Audio & Speed Controls
- **Renowned Arabic Reciters**: Switch between master reciters (including *Mishary Rashid Alafasy*, *Abdul Basit Abdul Samad*, *Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary*, *Abu Bakr Al-Shatri*, and *Saad Al-Ghamdi*).
- **Flexible Audio Modes**: Listen to **Arabic + Translation**, **Arabic Only**, or **Translation Only**.
- **Speed & Auto-Scroll**: Adjust playback speed (`0.75x`, `1.0x`, `1.25x`, `1.5x`) and enable smooth auto-scroll to keep active verses in focus.

### 5. 🎙️ 100% Private Written & Voice Reflections
Attach personal reflections, thoughts, and questions directly to any Ayah:
- **Written Notes**: Format and capture insights alongside the text.
- **Voice Notes**: Record spoken reflections directly onto the verse.
- **Total Local Privacy**: All notes, voice recordings, and bookmarks remain strictly on your physical device.

### 6. 🚶 Screen-Off Background Audio
Designed to fit naturally into everyday motion:
- Working out at the gym
- Commuting on the bus, train, or metro
- Driving through traffic
- Taking a quiet evening walk

Persistent lock-screen media controls and bluetooth headphone integration let you navigate verses while keeping your phone tucked away.

### 7. 🔔 Smarter Notifications & Settings
- **Intuitive Onboarding**: Setup your reminder preferences and grant permissions naturally during initial launch.
- **Permission Recovery**: Real-time permission status detection with direct recovery actions if notifications are turned off.
- **Notification Previews**: Test reminder notifications directly inside Settings.
- **Organized Architecture**: Sectional settings layout for seamless management of languages, reciters, typography, and storage.

## Privacy & Local Data

- **No Account Required**: No sign-ups, email capture, passwords, or cloud user profiles.
- **100% On-Device Study**: Written reflection notes, voice memos, bookmarks, and search queries are stored strictly in local device storage and are **never** transmitted to any server.
- **Anonymous Product Analytics**: Qurus uses PostHog solely for minimal, privacy-conscious metrics (such as active installs and app stability) using a random non-PII identifier. See our [Analytics Guide](docs/ANALYTICS.md) for full details.
- **In-App Feedback**: Submit bug reports and suggestions directly from Settings without an account.
