# Qurus Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 6, 2026
**Last Updated:** September 6, 2026

Qurus (“Qurus,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Quran reading, listening, and personal reflection application created by Hamdan Khubaib.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Qurus handles, how that information is stored and used, and what happens when the application communicates with external services.

Our goal is simple: your personal study activity should remain personal.

## 1. Information We Do Not Require

Qurus does not require you to create an account.

We do not require your name, email address, phone number, password, profile, or social-media account in order to use the application.

Qurus does not provide a social feed, public profile, comments system, or user-to-user messaging service.

## 2. Personal Study Data Stored on Your Device

Qurus allows you to create and maintain personal study information, including:

* Bookmarks
* Verse highlights
* Written reflection notes
* Voice notes
* Recently studied verses
* Your last studied location
* Reading streak information
* Reading and audio preferences
* Onboarding state

The current Qurus application stores this information locally on your device using on-device storage. The application does not provide a server-side account or cloud synchronization system for this information.

Your written reflections and voice recordings are therefore intended to remain on the device on which you created them.

Deleting the application, clearing its app data, changing devices, or otherwise removing local application storage may permanently remove locally stored study information.

Qurus cannot recover local study information that has been deleted or lost from your device.

## 3. Voice Notes and Microphone Access

Qurus includes an optional voice-note feature.

When you choose to record a voice note, Qurus requests permission to use your device microphone. The microphone is used to create the recording you explicitly request.

Voice recordings are associated with the relevant verse and stored locally on your device. Qurus does not operate a server that receives or stores your voice-note recordings as part of the current application architecture.

You can remove a voice note from the application through the available note controls.

You are responsible for ensuring that anything you record is appropriate for your intended use and does not unlawfully capture another person's private communications or information.

## 4. Reading Activity and Local Usage Information

Qurus maintains local application state necessary to provide features such as:

* Resuming your last reading position
* Recently studied history
* Bookmarks and highlights
* Reading streaks
* Reading preferences
* Audio preferences

This information is used to operate and personalize the application on your device.

The current application does not include a user analytics account, advertising profile, behavioral advertising system, or server-side reading-history database.

## 5. Search and Reading Interactions

Qurus allows you to search for Surahs and verse references.

The current search implementation performs searches against Quran data packaged with the application. Search input is therefore not designed to be transmitted to a Qurus-operated analytics service.

Qurus does not intentionally build a server-side profile of which Surahs, verses, or searches you use.

## 6. Network Requests and Third-Party Infrastructure

Although personal study data is designed to remain local, Qurus is not completely network-isolated.

Certain features require network access to retrieve remote resources, including Quran recitation audio, Urdu translation audio, artwork, and application updates.

For example, the current application retrieves audio resources from the EveryAyah domain and uses an external image resource for player artwork. The application is also configured to use Expo's update infrastructure for application updates.

When your device requests an external network resource, the relevant service provider may receive ordinary technical information associated with that request, such as an IP address, request time, device/network information, or other information normally processed by web infrastructure.

Qurus does not control the privacy practices, retention periods, or security practices of independent third-party infrastructure providers.

You should review the privacy documentation of relevant third-party providers where appropriate.

## 7. Device Permissions

Qurus may request device permissions necessary for specific features.

In particular, microphone permission is requested when you choose to record a voice note.

Qurus does not request access to your contacts, photos, address book, or location as part of the current core application functionality.

You can manage permissions through your device's operating-system settings.

## 8. Sharing Content

Qurus includes a system sharing feature for verses.

When you choose to share a verse, the application passes the selected verse content to the sharing functionality provided by your device and operating system.

Qurus does not operate the external application or service you choose for sharing, and content shared outside Qurus becomes subject to the policies of that external service.

## 9. Advertising and Tracking

Qurus is designed without advertisements.

The current Qurus application does not intentionally use behavioral advertising, cross-app advertising identifiers, or a Qurus-operated user tracking profile.

Qurus also does not sell users' personal information.

## 10. Data Retention

Because personal study information is stored locally, Qurus generally does not maintain a remote copy of your notes, bookmarks, highlights, or study history.

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the relevant application data, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove it through your device.

Remote infrastructure used for audio, artwork, or application updates may retain technical request information according to that provider's own policies and retention practices.

## 11. Security

Qurus is designed to minimize the collection of personal information by keeping user study data on-device.

However, no software, device, storage system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, including any device lock, operating-system security controls, backups, and access permissions.

Because Qurus does not provide cloud synchronization for your personal study data, losing access to your device may also mean losing locally stored information.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Qurus is not designed to require users to provide personal information, and the application does not knowingly collect account information from children.

Parents and guardians should nevertheless supervise their children's use of mobile applications and device permissions where appropriate.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Qurus outside the ordinary functionality of the application, please contact us so that we can investigate.

## 13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning personal data.

Because Qurus's current architecture stores personal study data locally rather than in a Qurus-operated account database, many requests concerning that information can be handled directly on the device by deleting or changing the relevant local data.

Where applicable law provides additional rights, including rights concerning access, correction, deletion, consent, complaints, or other personal-data matters, you may contact us using the contact information below.

Where Indian data-protection law applies, Qurus intends to operate its personal-data practices in accordance with applicable requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules and commencement provisions.

## 14. Third-Party Services

The current application relies on third-party infrastructure and libraries to provide parts of its functionality.

These may include:

* Expo / Expo Updates infrastructure
* EveryAyah-hosted audio resources
* External image-hosting resources
* Apple, Google, Android, iOS, and other operating-system services used to deliver device functionality

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

Qurus does not control the data practices of those independent providers.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Qurus changes, or when legal or regulatory requirements change.

When we make material changes, we may update the “Last Updated” date above and provide additional notice where appropriate.

Your continued use of Qurus after an updated policy becomes effective means the updated policy applies to your use of the application, to the extent permitted by applicable law.
